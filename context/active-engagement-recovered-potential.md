## Current configuration

R10 reflects a no-container, onsite-assembly configuration: JE designs and fabricates at its
Spokane facility, then assembles at Recovered Potential's California pilot site on an open
structural frame under a shed.

## Key pricing finding

The onsite (JE-assembles, "**B1**") configuration costs *more* than the container version — the
container itself is a modest line item, but travel and onsite labor inefficiency outweigh that
savings. The real budget lever for Recovered Potential is **Variant B2** (Recovered Potential
self-performs assembly).

A standalone SOW redline discussion draft was produced to frame the B1 vs. B2 decision ahead of
a meeting with Jinyu.
