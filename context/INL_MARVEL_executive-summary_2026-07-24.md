## The opportunity

Idaho National Laboratory's MARVEL is an 85 kWth demonstration microreactor built as a roughly two-year industry test bed, and INL is recruiting companies to run application demonstrations on it [1][2]. Johnston Engineering (JE) is not a nuclear operator, so the fit is not as a reactor end user. It is as the **hardware and engineering partner to an end-user team**, building the **non-nuclear balance-of-plant** every demonstration needs: heat-rejection skids, secondary-loop hardware, microgrid-integration equipment, process skids that use MARVEL's above-300 C heat, test rigs, and the non-nuclear controls replica INL already uses [1][2]. That work is JE's thermal, fluid, and test core, and it stays clear of the fission boundary.

## Status and timing

- The first Expression of Interest round **closed 08/30/2025**; INL has already competitively selected **five end-user teams** (reported participants include AWS and GE Vernova plus oil-and-gas majors; test cases include desalination, remote operations, and advanced sensors) [1][3].
- Next entry point: a **Notice of Opportunity expected in CY2026**, followed by a feasibility phase (FY2026) and physical demonstration (FY2027-2028), subject to appropriation [1][3].
