# Workflow 9 — National Lab IP & technology scouting

Trigger: "scout lab IP", "find lab tech we could commercialize", "what's
available from the labs", "tech-transfer scan"; also part of the monthly
cadence. The portals (step 2) and fit rubric (step 3) are inline below;
`references/funding-sources.md` supplies the keyword tiers and the
Ames→TdVib license-then-fund template.

JE's mission is commercializing first-of-a-kind tech, so lab IP that needs
mechanical/thermal/fluidic engineering to climb TRL is a JE engagement, a
teaming hook for a lab-partnered STTR/CRADA, or a license-then-fund play (the
Ames→TdVib template in `references/funding-sources.md`). Hold JE's core-IP
line the whole way: JE builds the hardware and systems around the science; the
lab keeps the proprietary chemistry.

1. Pick 1–2 lanes to scout (rotate; favor lanes tied to a live funding item or
   an active account).
2. Search the portals for available technologies in the lane: cross-lab first
   (Lab Partnering Service labpartnering.org; FLC Business
   flcbusiness.federallabs.org), then DoD (TechLink techlinkcenter.org), then
   per-lab catalogs (INL, PNNL, NREL→NLR, LLNL, LBNL, Sandia, ORNL, Argonne
   ACCESS/ReCell, Ames/CMI). Cross-check DOE patent releases.
3. Score each candidate on the lab-IP fit rubric (keep only if it clears all
   four: hardware/first-of-a-kind equipment; in a JE lane per the keyword
   tiers (tier ≥ 4; a tier-3 lane clears only when the hardware-build case
   is strong); advancing it needs mechanical/thermal/fluidic/DFM/test
   work; TRL ~3–7). Down-weight chem-only novelty with no hardware to build
   around, and anything already productized (TRL 8–9).
4. Apply the core-IP filter explicitly: JE's angle is the
   reactor/cell/stack/flow-field/BOP/thermal/packaging/test hardware and the
   structural-geometric form factor of electrodes and membranes — never the
   catalyst/electrolyte/membrane *formulation* or the reaction chemistry. If
   the only way to advance the tech is to change its chemistry, mark it
   licensee-led (a client licenses it; JE builds their hardware), not a
   JE-core play.
5. First screen the lane before proposing a play — lab catalogs (INL, ORNL,
   Argonne, PNNL) run heavy on nuclear and defense IP, and
   fission/HALEU/nuclear-fuel-cycle or ITAR/export-controlled tech carries
   the skill's standard compliance gate: flag it and route the go/no-go to
   Andy rather than shortlisting it as a clean JE play. Map each survivor to
   a play: (i) JE-teamed STTR/CRADA with the originating
   lab (STTR requires a research-institution partner — a lab qualifies); (ii)
   license-then-fund (a JE account or JE licenses the IP → eligible for
   SCALEUP/STTR as licensee); (iii) direct engagement where an existing
   account already holds or wants the license. Tie each to a funding hook from
   the board where one exists.
6. **Verify availability before anything is pitched or boarded.** TRLs from
   search snippets are estimates — mark them "(est.)" and say what confirms
   them. Portal listing pages are often JS-rendered: when details come from
   snippets rather than the actual page, flag the tech "needs click-through"
   rather than presenting it as confirmed. For any tech headed to a best-play
   or board proposal, name the licensing contact and the availability check
   to run first (e.g., email the lab's licensing office; some CMI/ORNL IP is
   already non-exclusively licensed — say to whom, since an existing licensee
   may itself be the JE prospect — and note whether the license is exclusive
   or non-exclusive: an already-exclusive license kills the license-then-fund
   play (ii) for a new account, leaving only direct engagement (iii) with the
   existing holder).
7. Shortlist ≤ 8/run:
   `Tech + lab | TRL (est.) | what it is | JE angle (hardware/analysis, core-IP-safe) | play | availability (licensing contact / "needs click-through" / already licensed to X) | portal link`.
   Propose next steps; do not write CRM or board items unless the operator
   says to (if a play maps to a real account or open solicitation, flag it for
   Workflow 2 or 1).
8. Digest: techs surfaced, best 1–2 plays with their availability-check next
   step, any that map to an existing account or open solicitation, portals
   still unscanned this rotation, and which shortlist rows carry
   "needs click-through" flags.
